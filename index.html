How to contribute

This place stays honest because the community maintains it. Presentation is simple, the rules are not.

1

Star & fork

Star the repository and fork it. Stars and forks are what keep the index alive and authentic.

2

Add a creator

Original makers and first crackers only — people who own their work. Pull request against the data files, profile link when one exists.

3

Name someone in the hell list

Every entry requires proof links — threads, receipts, screenshots, comparisons. No proof, no entry. Severity graded 0–10.

4

Fix, correct, question

Wrong link, missing credit, suspicion about an entry? Open an issue or send a correction. All entries are reviewed before they land.

Usage

Everything you need to know to use this registry well.

What is the Creators Registry?
A curated list of original people and first makers across the scene: individual legends (crackers, emulator and tool authors, uploaders), release groups, repackers, tools with their authors, hypervisor crews and the communities that hold it all together. Only real work gets a seat.
How does the Hall of Shame severity scale work?
Every entry is graded 0–10. 0–1 Low · 2–3 Moderate · 4–5 High · 6–7 Very High · 8–9 Brutal · 10 Super Risky. The grade is assigned at review from the submitted proofs and can be challenged with new evidence.
Where do the site pages get their data?
The website renders directly from data/creators.json and data/shame.json. Edit the data, push, and the site updates on its own. The matching .md files stay in sync as the human-readable copies.
What counts as proof in the hell list?
Public threads, receipts, screenshots, original-versus-stolen comparisons, server bans, admin logs. Proofs are gathered, can be expanded later, and each entry carries a status: contacted, verified, or pending.
Who decides what gets in?
Maintainers review every pull request and issue before anything lands. No vote farming, no unverified claims, no anonymous vendettas without evidence. Misuse gets a hell list entry of its own.
How do I report a wrong or harmful entry?
Open an issue with the evidence. If a creator was added by mistake, a link broke, or a shame entry lacks proof, the issue is processed promptly and the record corrected.
