# Hall of Shame — The Hell List

Exposure with evidence. Every entry demands proof. No proof, no entry.

## Severity scale (0–10)

| Grade | Label | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1 | Low | petty disputes, drama, unproven gossip |
| 2–3 | Moderate | ripped posts, minor credit theft, sketchy behavior |
| 4–5 | High | scamming, stolen work, fake giveaways |
| 6–7 | Very High | repeat scammers, large-scale credit theft, impersonation |
| 8–9 | Brutal | major fraud, malware/crypto scams, ruining people's setups |
| 10 | Super Risky | the worst of the worst — full-scale fraud, safe passage for no one |

## Evidence policy

- Every entry must carry **proof links** — receipts, screenshots, threads, original-vs-stolen comparisons.
- Proofs are checked before an entry is merged. Status: `contacted` (proof gathered), `verified` (accepted), `pending` (under review).
- Handles and links only. No doxxing, no private addresses.
