# Matrix Of Leadership

A public registry of original creators — the pirates, builders, and artists who keep the scene moving — alongside a hall of shame for scammers and content stealers.

**Caution:** Nothing in this repository is legal advice. Claims about individuals are provided in good faith based on publicly available community reports; verify everything yourself.

## Sections

| Section | Purpose |
|---|---|
| [**Creators**](data/creators.md) | Original, verified makers — their channels, platforms, and signature work |
| [**Hall of Shame**](data/hall-of-shame.md) | Recorded scammers, thieves, and impersonators with evidence links |

The creators registry is compiled from the GitHub network (stars, followers, following, repo contributors, README credits) plus public scene research — see [data/creators.md](data/creators.md).

## Contributing

To nominate a creator or report a scammer, open an issue with:

1. The name/alias
2. Platform links
3. Evidence (screenshots, receipts, original-versus-stolen comparisons)

All entries are reviewed before being merged.
